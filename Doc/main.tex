\begin{abstract}
    We propose \texttt{MPSSM-PCG}, a learned preconditioner for
    graph-structured linear systems based on MPSSM
    \cite{ceniMessagePassingStateSpaceModels2025}. We evaluate the method as a
    preconditioner for PCG and as a learned long-range propagation operator for
    shallow GCNs. On graph-derived systems, \texttt{MPSSM-PCG} achives
    convergence results comparable to
     \texttt{NeuralIF}\cite{hausnerNeuralIncompleteFactorization2024} while
    requiring substantially lower setup time. In addition, we show that the
    learned operator improves long-range propgation on ECHO-SSSP, allowing
    shallow GCNs to match or outperform deeper local GCNs.
\end{abstract}
